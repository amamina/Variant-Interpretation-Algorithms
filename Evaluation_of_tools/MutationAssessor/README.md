**MutationAssesso**r interprets the evolutionary conservation of substituted amino acids across protein homologs to evaluate their functional impacts in proteins. It investigates mutations associated with conditions like cancer or missense polymorphisms. By using entropy as a measure of conservation in which the variability of residues at a given position is measured—low entropy suggests strong conservation and more functional importance. 

A large entropy difference suggests that the new substitution is rare or never observed in that position and has potential functional disruption. Conservational data is then converted to numerical score. Its functional impact score (FIS) ranges from 0 to 1; variants closer to 1 are more damaging (Reva et al., 2011).


Reference:

Reva, B., Antipin, Y., & Sander, C. (2011). Predicting the functional impact of protein mutations: application to cancer genomics. Nucleic Acids Research, 39(17), e118. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkr407
